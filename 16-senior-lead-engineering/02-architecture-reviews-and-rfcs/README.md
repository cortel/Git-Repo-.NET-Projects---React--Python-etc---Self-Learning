# architecture reviews and rfcs

Status: planned.

## Goal

Write a proposal another developer can review and challenge.

## Evidence of completion

- [ ] Include assumptions, alternatives, risks, diagrams and a small validating experiment.
- [ ] Record feedback and why you accept or decline it.
- [ ] Define migration, ownership, success measures and revisit conditions.
- [ ] Document setup, exact run/check commands, and relevant failure cases.
- [ ] Explain an alternative and the cost of the chosen approach.


Keep implementation and evidence in this folder. Create src/, tests/, docs/, or infra/ when needed.
