## Goal

Compare a deterministic workflow, a single tool-using agent, and an optional multi-agent design for support triage.

## Evidence of completion

- [ ] Measure correctness, latency, cost, and failure recovery.
- [ ] Choose the simplest design that meets the requirements.
- [ ] Document setup, exact run/check commands, and relevant failure cases.
- [ ] Explain an alternative and the cost of the chosen approach.
